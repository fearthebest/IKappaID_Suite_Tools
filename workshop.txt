title=IKappaID Suite Tools
description=Build 42 admin suite — [b]v0.3.1.0[/b]. Claims, recovery journal, server utilities, and optional World Edit / Vehicles / Economy / Loot addons.
description=
description=[b]BETA[/b] means the suite is ready for real multiplayer playtesting on private servers. Core tools are usable; please share feedback in Discussions if something breaks — steps and console.txt help a lot.
description=
description=A clear control panel for multiplayer servers and the people who play on them — land and vehicle claims, recovery journal, and optional world tools. Matches the dark layout and orange highlights of [url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3749926419]IKappaID Phone Shop[/url].
description=
description=[b]Built for multiplayer[/b]
description=- Server-authoritative rules: claims, loot repop, and world edits run on the dedicated server; clients mirror what the server sends
description=- Designed for hosts who want one toolkit instead of juggling many single-purpose admin mods
description=- Sandbox options control what players can do themselves vs admin-only tools
description=
description=[b]What you get (base mod)[/b]
description=- Open the panel anywhere with [b]Ctrl+Shift+W[/b]
description=- Claim safehouses and vehicles; add members; choose who may enter, drive, or use storage
description=- Recovery journal: save your character on an item and restore skills and progress after death
description=- Server management: player care, weather and time, moderation, backups, and everyday utilities
description=- Sandbox settings for claim limits, guest access, and whether the journal is enabled
description=
description=[b]Optional addons[/b] — turn on only what your server needs
description=- [b]World Edit[/b] — paint and remove tiles, inspect builds, protect areas, copy blueprints, password locks for containers
description=- [b]Vehicles[/b] — list, move, repair, and remove vehicles (for hosts and trusted staff)
description=- [b]Economy[/b] — bank accounts, transfers, and player-run shops (works with IKappaID Phone Shop)
description=- [b]Loot[/b] — repopulate container loot with vanilla room distributions (admin); outdoor crates supported in BETA
description=
